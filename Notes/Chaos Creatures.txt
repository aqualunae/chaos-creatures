Blurb

Chaos Creatures is a pet collection game about using the power of randomness and friendship to save the world! Each creature has hundreds of possible variations: discover them through exploration or grow your team by pairing creatures and hatching their eggs.

Lovingly inspired by DeviantArt original species from the early 2000s, you can find the creature generation preview [on my portfolio](https://aqualunae.ca/sample/chaos/).

premise

you and your two best friends are going to summer camp, where you'll receive your first creatures. they'll be random adoptables for you, but rumour has it that the next year of kids will get standardized creatures from optima corp. your friends don't agree about whether that would be better or worse.

you get your creatures at camp and spend a while learning the basics of creature care and combat. once your councellor is confident that you can handle yourself, you're allowed to travel around the city.

during your travels, you meet a variety of people and their creatures, with different passions and ways of doing things. you also repeatedly run into employees and recruiters for optima corp, who become increasingly aggressive as you defeat and dismiss them.

the president of optima corp started as a pedigree creature breeder. he invested a lot into creating the perfect creature, but the result, in his eyes, was a failure. releasing the creature into the wild, he vows to create not just one perfect creature, but bring the entire species under control.

the failed creature who was his first attempt appears throughout the game, offering warnings. you can befriend it towards the end.
